These {n} images are one {span:.0f}-second video segment, sampled across it in time order.

Label it for a search index: what someone would type to find this clip again, not a description of the picture.

Reply with one line and nothing else: the five parts below, comma-separated, in this order. Every part is filled.

- action: what happens, named as a thing rather than as someone doing it: "cutting the cake", not "person cutting the cake". 2-6 words. If nothing happens, the scene's state: "empty car park".
- who: nobody, a woman, a man, a girl, a boy, two people, a man and a woman, a group.
- scene: where it is, 2-8 words, concrete enough to search: "beach with a stone pier", not "outdoors".
- light: one of: sunny, overcast, golden hour, dusk/dawn, night, floodlit, indoors.
- move: the parkour or freerunning move's own name, only when the move is unmistakable: a kong vault, a swing gainer, a wall run. 1-4 words. Otherwise "none"; never guess a name.

Plain words a person would type: "bike", not "two-wheeled pedal vehicle". No sentences, no framing ("a video showing", "the image shows"), no comment on the shot itself. Name only what you see, and write "unclear" for a key the stills cannot answer.
